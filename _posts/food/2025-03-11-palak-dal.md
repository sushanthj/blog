## Ingredients

### Pressue Cook
- **Tomato** : 1 (uncut)
- **Toor Dal** : 1/2 cup
- **Masoor Dal** : 1/4 cup
- **Garlic** : 4 cloves
- **Onion** : 1/4 cup
- **Green Chilly** : 3
- **Turmeric** : 1/2 teaspoon
- **Sesame Oil** : 1/4 teaspoon

### Tadka (Stir Fry)
- **Mustard** : 1/2 teaspoon
- **Cumin** : 1 teaspoon
- **Green Chilly** : 2
- **Red Chilly (Dried)** : 2
- **Curry Leaves** : 6
- **Garlic** : 3 cloves
- **Onion** : 1/4 cup

NOTE: Since onion is used in both stages you'll need a total of 1/2 cup of onion.

## Instructions

### Pressue Cooking Phase

Mix all ingredients mentioned above in a pressure cooker with 2.5 cups of water
- Pressure cook fro 4 whistles
- Remove tomato and blanch
- Re-add tomato and mash well when hot

### Tadka Phase

In a different vessel add oil and all the ingredients mentioned above. Fry until onions turn transparent.

### Mixing Phase

- Cut 100g of palak and add to the pressure cooker
- Add the tadka phase contents
- Add 1 cup of water and start to boil
- Add 1 teaspoon of tamarind concentrate (or 1/4 of tamarind water)
- Add 1 teaspoon of salt
- Add 1/2 teaspoon of red chilly powder
- Boil for 6-8 minutes
