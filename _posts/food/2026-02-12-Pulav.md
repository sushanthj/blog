## Ingredients

Green Paste (*contents will go in blender*)
- **Mint** : 1/2 cup
- **Cilantro** : 1/2 cup
- **Garlic** : 5 cloves
- **Ginger** : 2 inches
- **Green** Chilly : 2
- **Cloves** : 1/2 teaspoon
- **Cinnamon** : 2 inch (2 teaspoon of powder)
- **Cardamom** : 4 pods
- **Black Pepper** : 1/2 teaspoon

Takda Phase
- **Ghee** : 2 Tablespoon
- Bay Leaf : 2
- Cumin : 1 teaspoon
- Cashew : 1/4th cup
- Onion : 2 (sliced, not fine or diced)
- Carrot : 1 diced
- Potato : 1 diced
- Peas : 3 Tablespoon
- Beans : 10 (chopped to ~1 inch slices)
- Salt : 1/2 teaspoon
- Basmati Rice : 1.5 cup


## Instructions

### Blending Phase

Add all the ingredients mentioned in the *Green Paste* phase and 1/2 cup of water. Blend until it's a paste.

### Tadka Phase

1. Add 2 tablespoon of ghee
2. Add bay leaves, cumin and cashew and fry till cashew is light brown
3. Add onions, carrots, potato, peas, beans, salt
4. Satue for 1 minute
5. Then add the green paste and sautee for 2 minutes
6. Finally add 3 cups of water and bring to boil

### Cooking Phase
1. Add Basmati rice (pre-soaked for 5)
2. Add the cashew nuts
3. Blend until the cashew is thoroughly dissolved (cashew chunks mess up texture)
4. Add salt 1 teaspoon
5. Squeeze a little lemon if it helps
