## Ingredients

**Treble**

### Spice Water
- **Ginger** : 1 inch
- **Garlic** : 11 cloves
- **Mint** : 1 cup
- **Coriander** : 1.5 cup
- **Green Chilly** : 12
- **Pani Puri Masala** : 2 Tablespoon

### Sweet Chutney
- **Grated Jaggery** : 1 cup
- **Date Syrup** : 3/4 cup
- **Tamarind Concentrate** : 1/2 cup

**Bass**

### Puri Filling
- **Potato** : 4
- **Peas** : 1 cup

## Instructions

### Spice Water

- Mix all ingredients mentioned with 1 cup of water. Grind to paste.
- Then dilute paste with 3 more cups of water 

### Sweet Chutney

- Mix all ingredients mentioned above with 4 cupt sof water and heat until it starts boiling

### Puri Filling

- Pressure cook the potatoes
- Mash the potatoes with a little salt and pani puri masala
- Add peas and gently mix (try not to break the peas)
